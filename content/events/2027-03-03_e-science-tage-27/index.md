DMP4NFDI will be at the E-Science-Tage 2027, taking place from 3 to 5 March 2027 under the theme "*Breaking Barriers, Building Bridges*". 

We will offer the hands-on workshop, "**Prompt Your View: Creating Customised RDMO Outputs with AI Support**", introducing participants to the creation of RDMO Views using large language models (LLMs) and a newly released plugin. Through practical exercises, participants will learn how to create and customise Views by experimenting with different models, prompts and output templates. The workshop will also briefly address data protection considerations and share insights from current institutional practices. 

The workshop builds on the developed [Train-the-Trainer concept for DMPs and RDMO](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15771036). Prior experience of creating views and developing catalogues in RDMO is advantageous. Participants are asked to bring a laptop.

The session is organised by the DMP4NFDI basic service for the National Research Data Infrastructure (NFDI) and the Max Weber Stiftung.
