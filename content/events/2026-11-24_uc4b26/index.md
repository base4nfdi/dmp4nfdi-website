**DMP4NFDI will be present at the 3rd Base4NFDI User Conference - UC4B2026!**

This year again, Base4NFDI and the funded basic services want to get into discussion with you, our users.

Meet us at the [Poster Session](https://events.gwdg.de/event/1337/sessions/1945/#20261124) and the [Incubators Consultation Hour](https://events.gwdg.de/event/1337/contributions/4776/) to learn about our service portfolio and how we can together build and enhance standardised Data and Software Management Plan services across NFDI.

Curious about what collaborating with DMP4NFDI looks like in practice? Hear from consortia as they share their experiences and insights:

- Tuesday, 24. November: [DMP4PS: Building Shared Data Management Planning Support for the Physical Sciences](https://events.gwdg.de/event/1337/contributions/5295/)
- Wednesday, 25. November: [Leveraging the DMP4NFDI Base Service for FAIRagro to benefit real-life RDM](https://events.gwdg.de/event/1337/contributions/5329/)

⏰ When: **November 24-25, 2026**

🏛️ Where: **Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin** – Haus Potsdamer Straße, Potsdamer Straße 33, 10785 Berlin

***About the UC4B***

*The aim of the event is to present the basic services and the progress of their work, to shed light on the development and sustainable operation of research infrastructures in general and also from an international perspective. And, above all, to examine hands-on in cooperation with the basic service teams how the roll-out of basic services in the consortia can function technically and organizationally.*
