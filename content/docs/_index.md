DMP4NFDI is a Basic Service that supports NFDI consortia in developing and providing Data and Software Management Plan (DMP/SMP) services for their research communities.

This section brings together practical resources to help you get started and make the most of our services: 

🔎 Browse our **[frequently asked questions](/docs/faq)** and **[RDMO documentation](/docs/rdmodoc)** for guidance and answers to common questions. 

🧩 Explore our **[current collaborations with NFDI consortia](/docs/nfdi-overview)**. 

✨ Are you looking for support in creating Data Management Plans? Discover the **[DMP services available across the NFDI](/docs/nfdi-overview/#dmp-services-available-across-nfdi-consortia)**.
